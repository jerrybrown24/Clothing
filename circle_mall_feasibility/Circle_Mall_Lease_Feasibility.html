Rent history at Circle Mall

Until 2025-26 the store paid turnover rent of 14% of sales. The new rent of AED 246/sq ft (AED 885k) is about 1.7× what the store paid under turnover rent (AED 529k in the last lease year).

Benchmark: other Cotton On stores

Sales are the trailing 12 months, Sep-25 to Aug-26, from the Cotton On monthly file. Circle Mall makes AED 1,092 per sq ft. Dubai Hills makes AED 1,722 at a rent-to-sales ratio of 19.5%. At AED 246 on current sales, Circle Mall's rent-to-sales ratio is 22.5%. That is above Dubai Hills but below Mirdif and Dubai Mall.

Sales forecast

Eight methods were run on 32 months of Circle Mall sales (Jan-24 to Aug-26), plus sales and footfall from every Cotton On store in the region. Each method was trained to Dec-25 and tested on Jan–Aug 26. They were then combined, weighted by accuracy. From lease year 2, growth blends the model trend with UAE market, target-segment and JVC catchment drivers.

Circle Mall vs the rest of the chain

Sales growth, Jan–Aug 26 vs Jan–Aug 25, for stores that traded in every month.

Most destination malls fell sharply in 2026 (Dubai Mall −19%, DCC −21%, Ibn Battuta −20%). Circle Mall grew 14%. A community mall with residents within walking distance held up better than tourist-dependent malls. That supports keeping the store, but it is one strong year, so the base case does not extrapolate it.

Simulator

Rent simulator

Enter a gross rent per sq ft and pick a lease year. Sales, margins and every other cost come from the P&L above, and only the rent changes. Rent is applied as a flat gross amount (rate × 3,598 sq ft).

Income is Net Income (sales after gross margin and margin deductions). Expense is every store operating cost including rent. Cash profit is store profit before depreciation and finance charges. PAT is cash profit less depreciation, finance charges and tax. The 9% default is the UAE corporate tax rate, applied at store level as an illustration; set it to 0 to see profit before tax. Depreciation defaults to the P&L figure for the year, and you can overwrite it.

Sources: Circle_Mall_002.xlsx (P&L, P&L 5 Yrs, Scenario, Circle Mall Rent, notes, YR'26) and COG_month-loc_salesfootfall_from_2024.xlsx (sales and footfall, all Cotton On stores). Market inputs: Grand View Research apparel outlook (MEA CAGR 4.3%, 2026–33), Dubai Data & Statistics / WAM (Dubai population 4.58M at end-2025, +7.5%), public JVC community guides (≈90–100k residents, ≈300k+ at build-out).
Models: Holt-Winters and SARIMA (statsmodels), Ridge, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting (scikit-learn). Reproduce with forecast.py and pnl.py in this folder.

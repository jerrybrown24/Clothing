Section 1

Rent, rent-to-sales and rent-to-income

Rent options for the new term

Gross rent = base rent + service charge + marketing levy, per sq ft per year, × 3,598 sq ft. Turnover rent is 12% of sales, charged only if it exceeds base rent.

Ratios by option

Rent-to-sales is gross rent ÷ net sales. Rent-to-income is gross rent ÷ Net Income, the P&L line after gross margin and margin deductions (64.0% of sales). Bands used: rent-to-sales ≤20% healthy, 20–24% stretched, >24% high. Rent-to-income ≤32% healthy, 32–38% stretched, >38% high.

Rent history at Circle Mall

Until 2025-26 the store paid turnover rent of 14% of sales. The P&L moved to a fixed rent of AED 246/sq ft (AED 885k) from 2026. The landlord's proposal of AED 296 is +20% on that and roughly 2× what the store paid under turnover rent.

Benchmark: other Cotton On stores

Sales are the trailing 12 months, Sep-25 to Aug-26, from the Cotton On monthly file. Circle Mall makes AED 1,092 per sq ft. Dubai Hills makes AED 1,722 at a rent-to-sales ratio of 19.5%. At AED 296 on current sales, Circle Mall's rent-to-sales ratio would be 27.1%, the highest of these stores.

Sales forecast

Eight methods were run on 32 months of Circle Mall sales (Jan-24 to Aug-26), plus sales and footfall from every Cotton On store in the region. Each method was trained to Dec-25 and tested on Jan–Aug 26. They were then combined, weighted by accuracy. From lease year 2, growth blends the model trend with UAE market, target-segment and JVC catchment drivers.

Backtest and results by method

MAPE = mean absolute % error against actual Jan–Aug 26 sales, which none of the models saw. Ensemble weight = 1/MAPE, normalised. Models with MAPE above 25% would get zero weight; none did.

Circle Mall vs the rest of the chain

Sales growth, Jan–Aug 26 vs Jan–Aug 25, for stores that traded in every month.

Most destination malls fell sharply in 2026 (Dubai Mall −19%, DCC −21%, Ibn Battuta −20%). Circle Mall grew 14%. A community mall with residents within walking distance held up better than tourist-dependent malls. That supports renewing, but it is one strong year, so the base case does not extrapolate it.

Full P&L and recommendation

Costs are carried unchanged from the existing P&L (the Yr'27, Yr'28 and Yr'29 lines of the "P & L 5 Yrs" sheet): gross margin 74.0%, Net Income 64.0% of sales, and the same employee, marketing, opex and shared-cost amounts with +2% a year. Only sales and rent change. Years run on the lease calendar (Aug–Jul).

Scenario 2 follows the mall's renovation requirement: AED 2.0M capex, depreciated straight-line over 5 years (AED 400k a year). Over a 3-year term, AED 800k of book value is left at expiry. That is only recovered if the lease is renewed again.

Section 1

Rent, rent-to-sales and rent-to-income

Rent schedule and ratios by scenario (3 years)

Gross rent = base rent + service charge + marketing levy, per sq ft per year, × 3,598 sq ft. Service charge (AED 41) and marketing levy (AED 15) are held at today's level in both scenarios, so base rent is the rest. Sales follow the adopted assumption: year 1 forecast, year 2 flat, year 3 −10%. The first row is the last year of the old lease, for comparison.

Rent-to-sales is gross rent ÷ net sales. Rent-to-income is gross rent ÷ Net Income, the P&L line after gross margin and margin deductions (64.0% of sales). Bands used: rent-to-sales ≤20% healthy, 20–24% stretched, >24% high. Rent-to-income ≤32% healthy, 32–38% stretched, >38% high.

Rent history at Circle Mall

Until 2025-26 the store paid turnover rent of 14% of sales. The new rent of AED 246/sq ft (AED 885k) is about 1.7× what the store paid under turnover rent (AED 529k in the last lease year).

Benchmark: other Cotton On stores

Sales are the trailing 12 months, Sep-25 to Aug-26, from the Cotton On monthly file. Circle Mall makes AED 1,092 per sq ft. Dubai Hills makes AED 1,722 at a rent-to-sales ratio of 19.5%. On current sales, AED 246 gives a rent-to-sales ratio of 22.5% and AED 276 gives 25.3%. That is above Mirdif (25.1%) and close to Dubai Mall (25.8%). Year 3's AED 304 on 10% lower sales reaches 29%.

    Circle Mall vs the rest of the chain

    Sales growth, Jan–Aug 26 vs Jan–Aug 25, for stores that traded in every month.

    Most destination malls fell sharply in 2026 (Dubai Mall −19%, DCC −21%, Ibn Battuta −20%). Circle Mall grew 14%. A community mall with residents within walking distance held up better than tourist-dependent malls. That supports keeping the store, but it is one strong year, so the base case does not extrapolate it.

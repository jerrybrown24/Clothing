        How this was worked out

        • Source: GfK UAE panel, August 2026 (Sheet1, 19,805 model rows). Market = GfK panel market; Chains = tracked chains, which include SDG. Values are assumed to be AED.
        • Fair share for a brand = sum over its product groups of (brand's market value × SDG's share of that product group). Share vs fair = SDG value ÷ fair share. 1.0× means SDG sells the brand at its normal rate.
        • Onboard: no SDG sales, market value ≥ AED 200k. High priority = opportunity ≥ AED 100k and ≥ 50% sold through chains. Medium = opportunity ≥ AED 40k. Watch = the rest.
        • Improve: SDG sales > 0, market value ≥ AED 500k, share vs fair < 0.8×. Fix or exit < 0.1×, Major gap < 0.4×, Close gap otherwise.
        • Remove: SDG sales < AED 15k (or net negative), share vs fair < 0.35×, market value < AED 1M, and not already on the Improve list.
        • This is one month. Launch timing, stock-outs, seasonality (air conditioning peaks in summer) and exclusive supplier deals can move any single brand. Confirm with 3 to 6 months of GfK data and SDG's own stock and margin figures before delisting.
        • Feature phones (Nokia, Benco) show as gaps because SDG sells no feature phones at all. That is a category decision, not a brand one.

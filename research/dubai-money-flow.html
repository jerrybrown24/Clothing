Dubai property money flow

Four core routes plus two the original chart left out: off-plan resale and renting. Each route shows who pays whom, how much, at which step, and where your money sits while you wait. Change the price to recalculate everything.

Example figures. Off-plan uses a 10 / 50 / 40 payment plan. Ready mortgages use the expat first-home cap: 80% LTV up to AED 5m, 70% above.

Sources and assumptions

  1. DLD 4% transfer fee, AED 580 title deed fee, trustee fee AED 4,000 + VAT (AED 2,000 under AED 500k): Property Finder, DLD fees
  2. Mortgage registration 0.25% of loan + AED 290: Oliva, DLD mortgage registration
  3. Off-plan LTV capped at 50%; ready expat LTV 80% / 70%: Al Tamimi, UAE mortgage regulations; haus & haus, off-plan mortgages
  4. Escrow rules and 5% retention: Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007
  5. Form F and 10% security cheque: ATB Legal, resale SPA exit
  6. DubaiNow Digital Sale eligibility: DLD, buy or sell via DubaiNow
  7. Assignment thresholds and NOC fees: Oliva, assignment fees (indicative)
  8. Rental deposits, agency fee, DEWA deposit: fäm Properties renting guide (indicative)
  9. Assumptions: valuation AED 3,000 + VAT; bank processing shown at the 1% maximum; developer commission 4%; bank referral 0.5%; Ejari ≈ AED 220; DEWA activation ≈ AED 130. Confirm before quoting customers.

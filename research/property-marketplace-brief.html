Founder research brief · UAE PropTech

A "Buy Now" marketplace for UAE homes and rentals

An Amazon-style property marketplace where a buyer or tenant picks a unit, pays through one checkout, and the company handles everything after: the developer or seller, DLD registration, trustee, mortgage, Ejari and handover.

Prepared 1 Oct 2026Market data: DLD, ADREC, company filings, 2025 to H1 2026FX: USD 1 = AED 3.6725

01Executive summary

Verdict: feasible, if you change what "Buy Now" means

Start with off-plan in Dubai, stay asset-light, never hold client money.

The idea is not unique, but nobody owns it yet. Portals (Property Finder, Bayut) sell advertising to brokers. Huspy and PRYPCO own mortgages and agent tooling. Oliva, a small 2025-era startup, already offers remote end-to-end purchase for overseas investors. Nobody offers a true checkout across many developers plus resale and rentals.

The window opened in October 2025, when Dubai (DLD "Digital Sale" on DubaiNow) and Abu Dhabi (ADREC "Digital Buy & Sell Journey") made fully online transfer legally possible.19,20 The hard parts are regulation (brokers may not hold client funds), resale inventory (every unit needs a willing, pre-priced seller), and a market cycle that Fitch expects to correct by up to 15%.22,27

02SWOT analysis

Assessed for a new, asset-light "managed transaction" marketplace launching in Dubai in 2027.

SStrengths

Internal · what the model has going for it

  • Clear consumer pain. Today's journey is fragmented across portal, agent, developer, bank, trustee centre, DLD and DEWA. One checkout and one accountable party is a real product difference.
  • Buyer pays nothing on off-plan. Developers pay agents 3–7% of price, so a marketplace can be free to buyers and still earn about AED 65–87k per average deal.26
  • Off-plan inventory behaves like SKUs. Fixed price lists, floor plans, payment plans and live availability make a "Reserve now" button realistic.
  • Government rails now exist. UAE Pass e-signature, DubaiNow Digital Sale, ADREC digital journey, Ejari online and DLD-supervised escrow give you the plumbing without building it.
  • Asset-light. No balance-sheet property risk if you act as a licensed broker and orchestrator, not a principal buyer.
  • Fixed pricing transparency (all-in cost shown upfront: 4% DLD fee, trustee fee, agency fee, VAT) is rare today and builds trust.25

WWeaknesses

Internal · what you lack on day one

  • No supply. Developers already sell through 1,000+ brokerages and their own apps. You start with zero developer agreements and zero resale listings.
  • No traffic. Portals own buyer intent. Your early customer acquisition will likely run through the very portals you compete with, plus paid search.
  • "Pay my company" is not legally simple. Dubai brokers are not allowed to hold client money; funds go to developer escrow or the seller via the trustee process.22 Needs a payments partner or licence.
  • Mortgages break "instant". About 52% of Dubai sales by count were mortgage-backed in 2025.4 Pre-approval and valuation take days to weeks.
  • High ticket, low frequency. Average deal AED 1.8–3.7m; customers buy once every few years. Repeat purchase and brand recall are hard.
  • Operations-heavy. Each deal needs licensed people for snagging, handover, disputes and paperwork. Margins depend on ops efficiency.

OOpportunities

External · tailwinds you can ride

  • Record volumes. Dubai H1 2026 sales AED 286bn across 86,000 deals, second-best half on record.5
  • Remote foreign buyers. Foreigners are 85–88% of Dubai transactions by count; India, UK, Pakistan, Russia and China lead.29 They value a single trusted remote checkout most.
  • Trust gap. RERA logged about 3,800 property complaints in 2025, roughly 400 involving suspected fraud; portals are under pressure over misleading "verified" listings.30
  • Rentals. 513,000 new Dubai leases in 2025.7 "Rent now" with Ejari plus monthly payments (Keyper-style partner) is an underserved, frequent-use wedge.
  • Adjacent revenue. Mortgage referral, conveyancing, Golden Visa, furnishing, moving, DEWA setup, home insurance, property management.
  • Policy push. Dubai Real Estate Strategy 2033 targets AED 1 trillion in transactions and AED 60bn in tokenised property by 2033.32
  • Regional expansion. Abu Dhabi's fully digital journey; Saudi Arabia later (Huspy and Stake are already moving there).

TThreats

External · what can hurt you

  • Well-funded incumbents. Property Finder took USD 525m from Permira and Blackstone plus USD 250m Ares debt in 2025; it can add a checkout faster than you can build traffic.8
  • Developers going direct. Emaar's apps already let buyers browse, reserve and pay online. Tier-1 developers may refuse to pay a marketplace more than a broker.
  • Government as competitor. DLD and ADREC now offer free digital transfer. The "we handle DLD" message weakens over time.
  • Market cycle. Fitch forecast up to a 15% price correction through 2026, with about 120,000 completions in 2026 alone.27 Off-plan launches and commissions fall first in a downturn.
  • Regulatory change and AML. Brokers are DNFBPs under Federal Decree-Law 10 of 2025: KYC on every buyer, mandatory reports for cash ≥ AED 55,000 or any crypto, fines up to AED 5m per violation.24
  • Broker backlash. Brokerages are the portals' and developers' main customers; they will lobby against a channel that cuts them out.

What the SWOT implies

  • S + O: Lead with free-to-buyer off-plan checkout for remote foreign buyers, the segment with the most pain and the least need for a mortgage on day one.
  • W + O: Solve "pay my company" by orchestrating payments straight into DLD escrow and the trustee process through a licensed payments partner, never into your own account.
  • S + T: Be the trust layer (verified units, all-in pricing, one accountable party) rather than a cheaper portal. That is defensible against Property Finder's traffic advantage.
  • W + T: Do not touch iBuying (buying homes onto your balance sheet). Zillow lost over USD 1bn doing it.28

05Why this hasn't been executed yet

1. A home is not a SKU

Amazon works because a seller commits stock at a fixed price. Resale homes have one owner, negotiable prices, tenants inside, and sellers who change their mind. A "Buy now" button on resale only works if the seller has pre-signed a mandate at a fixed price.

So what: off-plan is SKU-like today; resale needs a "pre-priced, pre-verified" listing product first.

2. Brokers may not hold the money

Dubai brokers are not allowed to keep client funds; off-plan payments go into DLD-supervised developer escrow under Law 8 of 2007, and resale funds go seller-ward through the trustee centre.21,22 A company taking the buyer's whole payment needs a Central Bank payments licence or a regulated partner.

So what: design checkout as payment routing into escrow, not payment to you.

3. The portals' customers are brokers

Property Finder and Bayut earn most revenue from broker subscriptions and listing fees. A checkout that cuts brokers out would attack their own customers. This is a classic innovator's dilemma, so they add mortgage and data products instead.

So what: this is your opening, and the reason a startup can do what incumbents won't.

4. Developers control distribution

Brokerages drive an estimated ~70% of off-plan sales, and developers reward them with 3–7% commission and launch bonuses.26 A new channel has to prove volume before a tier-1 developer gives it inventory access.

So what: start with tier-2 developers who need distribution, then use volume to win tier-1.

5. The rails were not digital until late 2025

Before October 2025, transfer required in-person trustee visits and manager's cheques. The DLD Digital Sale and ADREC digital journey arrived in October 2025, and Dubai's version still excludes mortgaged deals and non-residents without Emirates ID.19,20

So what: this is the "why now". The full online loop is newly possible but still incomplete.

6. Mortgages and AML add friction

Half of deals by count need bank approval and valuation, and every buyer needs KYC, source-of-funds checks and possible reports to the FIU via goAML.4,24 Instant purchase is impossible for those buyers.

So what: "Buy now" should mean "reserve now, close in days", with a clear tracker.

What "Buy now" should mean in practice

Today (typical Dubai off-plan purchase)

  1. Browse a portal, enquire, get called by several agents
  2. Agent sends PDFs, price list may be stale
  3. Visit sales centre or video call
  4. Sign EOI, transfer booking deposit
  5. Sign SPA, pay down payment + 4% DLD fee
  6. Oqood registration chased by agent
  7. Instalments tracked by buyer in spreadsheets
  8. Handover, snagging, DEWA, Ejari or resale: on your own

Your product

  1. Verified unit page with live price, all-in cost and payment plan
  2. KYC with UAE Pass or passport e-KYC, in minutes
  3. Reserve now: deposit routed directly to developer escrow
  4. SPA e-signed; Oqood/DLD fee paid in the same checkout
  5. Order tracker: every instalment, construction progress, documents
  6. Optional mortgage, Golden Visa, furnishing, insurance add-ons
  7. Handover concierge: snagging, DEWA, title deed
  8. One-click "lease it out" or "resell" when ready

09Plan, milestones and actions

24 months, gated. Do not start a phase until the previous gate is met.

Phase 0Months 0–2

Validate and de-risk

  • Run the interviews and smoke test from §08
  • Get the payments and licensing legal opinion
  • Mystery-shop the 6 closest competitors
  • Approach 10 tier-2 developers; aim for 3 LOIs
  • Recruit a RERA-certified broker-manager co-founder or early hire
Gate: legal path confirmed · 3 developer LOIs · cost per qualified lead under AED 500 in at least one corridor
Phase 1Months 2–5

Set up and build the MVP

  • DET licence, RERA brokerage registration, office, goAML registration, bank account
  • Sign payments partner and 2 mortgage partners
  • Build MVP: verified unit pages, all-in price calculator, e-KYC, "Reserve now" with escrow routing, order tracker
  • Manual ops desk for inventory sync and SPA/Oqood paperwork
  • Raise pre-seed to cover 18 months (size it from your Phase 0 model)
Gate: licensed · 3 developers live with ≥ 300 units · first payment routed to escrow end-to-end in a test deal
Phase 2Months 5–9

Launch off-plan in Dubai

  • Go live for 2 overseas corridors plus UAE residents
  • Close the first 25 deals; measure CAC, time-to-SPA, NPS
  • Add 5 more developers, including one tier-1 pilot
  • Launch mortgage pre-approval inside checkout
Gate: 25 closed deals (≈AED 55m GMV, ≈AED 2m revenue) · CAC below 30% of net fee · NPS above 50
Phase 3Months 9–15

Add resale and rentals

  • Pilot "Buy now" resale: exclusive, pre-priced, inspected listings in 3 communities; close via DLD Digital Sale and trustee partner
  • Launch "Rent now": verified rentals, online Ejari, monthly payments with a partner
  • Post-handover services: leasing, property management, resale
  • Raise seed round on traction
Gate: 40 deals per month across lines · first 50 resale and 200 rental closings · repeat or referral share above 20%
Phase 4Months 15–24

Scale

  • Abu Dhabi launch on the ADREC digital journey
  • Developer API integrations replacing manual sync
  • Add more overseas corridors with local referral partners
  • Prepare Series A; evaluate Saudi Arabia
Gate: ≈170 deals per month run-rate (year-3 SOM pace) · contribution margin positive per deal

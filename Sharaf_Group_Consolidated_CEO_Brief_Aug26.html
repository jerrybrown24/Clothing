  • Sharaf DG is still converting a shrinking topline into a growing bottom line. DG YTD revenue of AED 1,867.06M is 96.4% of budget and -6.7% against last year — yet DG's YTD PAT of AED 50.78M is 108% of budget and +28.3% year on year.

    Chillout (183%), Dalma Mall zone (158%), Body Diera City Center (100%), Typo Dubai Mall (58%), Typo Mirdiff City Center (54%), Al Wasl Galleria zone (51%), Typo Spinneys (51%), AHQ Dalma Mall (51%), Al Barsha zone (46%), AHQ MCC (42%) together lose AED 9,688,797 YTD.     ≈ AED 2,973,222 annualised.

  • Extinguish the Fashion Studio finance charge

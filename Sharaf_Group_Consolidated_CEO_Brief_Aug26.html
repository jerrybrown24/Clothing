08 AI suggestions — Sharaf DG

Generated from the numbers in this pack. Each carries the specific figure that motivates it and an order-of-magnitude value, so the board can rank by size rather than by narrative.

  • Split Energy into two managed businesses at the September close

    Solar and Lighting are reported as one vertical with one budget line loaded entirely on Solar. That single presentation hides a AED 1.40M earner inside a AED -1.83M loss and makes the vertical's 81.5% budget achievement uninterpretable. Give each its own P&L, own budget and own owner.

    Cost: a reporting change. Value: management visibility over AED 18.83M of revenue currently steering blind.

  • Set a hard fixed-cost ceiling on Solar tied to the contracted order book

    Solar carries AED 5.17M of expense against AED 11.19M of revenue at a 17.0% gross margin — the gross margin earned is AED 1.90M against AED 5.17M of cost. Size the overhead to the signed book, not the aspiration.

    Closing even half the overhead gap is worth ~AED 2.45M annualised.

  • Run a conversion-recovery programme in the six largest UAE doors

    UAE conversion fell 1.8pp YTD (15.2% → 13.3%) and was 11.5% in August. Applied to YTD footfall of 5,241,355 and this year's ATV of AED 1,632, each recovered percentage point of conversion is worth roughly AED 86M of revenue over the same eight months.

    Recovering half the lost conversion ≈ AED 79M of eight-month revenue — an upper bound, since the marginal baskets a conversion recovery adds are typically smaller than the current average ticket.

  • Rebase the Oman budget to the continuing business

    The Corporate line fell AED 88.20M year on year at negligible margin, yet still sits in the budget at AED 46.41M. It makes the group look 95.9% of plan when the continuing business is materially closer to plan. Rebase it and report Oman on retail revenue.

    No P&L impact — removes a AED 49.42M distortion from every group revenue conversation.

  • Put a stop-loss gate on the two KSA stores

    Riyadh Gallery and Granada consumed AED -14.92M YTD on AED 12.87M of sales. The trajectory is improving (+28.4% YoY) but the run rate is still roughly AED 1.86M a month. Define the December revenue threshold now, while the decision is unforced.

    Avoided loss if the gate triggers: ~AED 22.37M annualised.

  • Fund Telecom expansion out of the KSA/Energy capital envelope

    Telecom returns AED 9.73M of PAT on AED 43.20M of revenue — a 22.5% net margin, 133% of budget and +32.3% YoY, with almost no working capital. It is the highest-return unit in the group and the least discussed.

    Every AED 10M of Telecom revenue added at current rates carries ~AED 2.3M of PAT.

  • Convert the back-end income outperformance into a formalised vendor programme

    Back-end income of AED 71.42M is AED 14.47M ahead of plan and +7.6% on last year — it is doing more to hold group profit than any operational lever. Branding income (AED 54.17M, 99% of plan) and event contribution (AED 23.80M, 62% of plan) are the two that are underperforming.

    Those three books hold AED 11.98M of the group's AED 12.17M past-90-day exposure. A single named owner with fortnightly reporting is the cheapest intervention in this list.

    Cash release potential: AED 5.99M.

    SDG Online (AED 35.21M, +74.7%) and E-Retail (AED 93.06M) together exceed any physical door, and online growth is the mirror image of the store conversion decline. Until the two are read together, it is impossible to tell whether the group is losing customers or moving them.

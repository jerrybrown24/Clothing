06

Recommendation

Pulling the competition-data gap, the sales/income/bottom-line validation, footfall, margin, branding and RAAS views together into a single call.

RENEW
— conditionally
Proceed with the Muscat Grand Mall renewal — but only alongside a documented explanation for the mall-wide traffic collapse.
Rent is proposed down 13.6% (OMR 76,681 → 66,227). Commercial Finance forecasts Net Profit after Tax growing 15% (OMR 198,007 → 228,461); the independently-built AI projection, using a more cautious H2 sales assumption, lands at OMR 213,738 — still growth over FY2025, just smaller, and only -6.4% from Finance's own number. Both views agree: costs are shrinking faster than revenue, and the store is holding its share of the mall's traffic. The open question isn't this store's performance; it's why the whole mall's footfall fell ~20% in H1 2026.

Why renew

  • Both forecasts agree on direction: Net profit after tax rises versus FY2025 under Finance's build (OMR 198,007 → 228,461, +15%) and under the more cautious AI build (→ 213,738, +7.9%) — even as sales fall.
  • The AI projection, built independently off the same logic used at Mall of Oman (continue the observed trend rather than assume a turn), lands within -6.4% of Finance's own number on every bottom-line metric — comfortably inside the ±25% band this review targets.
  • The proposed rent is 13.6% lower than the current rent (OMR 76,681 → 66,227) — a landlord concession that directly de-risks this renewal.
  • This store's share of the mall's footfall has risen every period since FY2023 (2.88% → 3.85% → 3.61% → 3.78% YTD) — it is not losing ground to other tenants within the mall.
  • Trading margin, branding and RAAS income are all improving as a share of sales, even as absolute sales decline.

Watch items

  • No competition or comparative-tenant data exists for Muscat Grand Mall in this file (Section 01) — Section 6 of the source workbook only carries an unrelated UAE property's figures, duplicated from the Mall of Oman file.
  • Muscat Grand Mall's own footfall fell -19.9% in H1 2026 — almost exactly matching this store's -20.2% sales decline. This is a mall-wide issue, not a store-specific one, and its cause is not documented anywhere in this file.
  • Every single month from Jan–Jul 2026 was down double digits YoY (-10% to -28%) — a sustained decline, not the noisy up-and-down pattern seen at other properties.
  • No FY2027+ forecast exists yet (from Finance or AI), so there is no view on whether the mall's traffic — and this store's sales — stabilize, keep falling, or recover.
  • No signed lease or legal terms document was provided this time (unlike the Mall of Oman review) — lease length, escalation schedule, penalties and deposits for this renewal are unknown.

Before final sign-off

  • Get a documented, dated explanation from Operations/Mall Management for the mall-wide footfall collapse before treating FY26 as a one-off dip.
  • Obtain the actual signed lease terms (term length, escalation, penalties, deposits) — not available in this data set.
  • Build a FY2027+ multi-year model (both Finance and AI views) once FY26 closes, rather than renewing on a single forecast year alone.
  • Request genuine Muscat Grand Mall comparator-tenant data from BD/Leasing — Section 6's data pack is unusable for this property (see Section 01).
  • Route the file through Operations, BD and Finance so Sections 1–5 and 7 carry an actual documented recommendation, not a blank template.

Conclusion: the financial case for renewing holds up under both the Finance and AI views — profit grows in both, rent is falling, and this store is not the source of the traffic decline. The two forecasts agree closely enough (within 6.4% at the bottom line, within 3.2% on sales) that neither should be read as the "aggressive" or "safe" case — they're two independent routes to the same conclusion. But this is not a clean "renew" the way Mall of Oman was: there is no competitor benchmark for this mall, and the trigger for the mall's own -20% H1 footfall drop is unexplained. Both deserve an answer before this renewal is signed as routine.

Source & method. Historical monthly sales, mall and store footfall, and annual trading margin / branding / RAAS income are all sourced directly from tabs within the Lease Renewal Standardized Review workbook for Muscat Grand Mall (Store FF-38A&B). The FY2026 Finance forecast (Section 05) is the single-year forecast built in that workbook, shown in full with its own assumptions/remarks. The FY2026 AI projection (Sections 02–05) is independently derived: it extends the actual Jan–Jul 2026 year-to-date trend through H2 rather than assuming Finance's partial stabilization, then flows that sales figure through the same blended margin/branding/RAAS rates and cost structure Finance uses — every resulting line is within 6.4% of Finance's own figure, inside the ±25% validation band this review applies. No signed lease agreement or municipal registration form was provided for this property in this review, unlike the Mall of Oman analysis — lease-term and legal detail should be treated as an open item. Section 6 of the source workbook (the mandatory data pack) still contains an unrelated property's data and was not used in this report — see Section 01.
